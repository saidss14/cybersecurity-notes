# CIA Triad

## Introduction

The CIA Triad is a fundamental cybersecurity model that forms the foundation of information security. It consists of three core principles:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

---

## 1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that information is accessible only to authorized users.

### Methods
- Encryption
- Passwords
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Access Control

### Threats
- Data Breaches
- Unauthorized Access
- Phishing Attacks

---

## 2. Integrity

Integrity ensures that data remains accurate, complete, and unaltered.

### Methods
- Hashing
- Digital Signatures
- Checksums

### Threats
- Data Tampering
- Malware
- Unauthorized Modifications

---

## 3. Availability

Availability ensures that systems and data are accessible when needed.

### Methods
- Backups
- Redundant Systems
- Disaster Recovery Plans

### Threats
- DDoS Attacks
- Hardware Failures
- Power Outages

---

## Real-World Example

Online Banking System:

- Confidentiality → Protects customer account information.
- Integrity → Ensures transactions cannot be altered.
- Availability → Keeps banking services accessible 24/7.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Confidentiality protects data from unauthorized access.
- Integrity ensures data accuracy and trustworthiness.
- Availability ensures systems and services remain operational.
- The CIA Triad is the foundation of cybersecurity.
